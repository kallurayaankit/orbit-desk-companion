# LinkedIn

Back to my electronics roots—with a little droid for company.

I have a bachelor's and a master's with research in electronics, and spent 3–4 years working in the industry during 2012–2018. Building Orbit has been a rather lovely way to connect that background with AI-assisted software development.

Orbit is an ESP32-S3 desk-companion concept you can explore in your browser:

• Five applications: everyday desk companion, focus timer, room climate, plant environment and a voice-interaction concept.
• Interactive 3D scenes, time-of-day controls and simulated sensor responses.
• A Circuit workshop for every application: assembled, exploded and PCB placement views.
• Component explanations, guided assembly order, pin-to-pin wiring, parts lists and downloadable Arduino diagnostic sketches.

I'm particularly chuffed with the connection between the visual prototype and the electronics underneath. You can look inside, select a component and understand why it's there.

This is a digital prototype and proposed build guide. The sensor and voice interactions are simulated; the circuit still needs physical validation before a real build.

Live demo: https://orbit-companion-dusky.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/kallurayaankit/orbit-desk-companion
